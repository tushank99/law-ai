### Subject: Demand for Refund of Security Deposit of Rs. 60,000  

---

#### 1.  Facts  

1.1. The parties entered into a written rent agreement for the residential flat situated at **[FLAT ADDRESS, Pune]** on **[date of agreement]**.  

1.2. As per the agreement, the tenant (the undersigned) paid a security deposit of **Rs. 60,000** to the landlord, Mr. Sharma, by bank transfer on **[date of deposit]**.  

1.3. The tenancy was lawfully terminated and the flat was vacated on **31 August 2026**. The tenant handed over vacant possession of the premises to the landlord on that date.

1.5. Despite repeated verbal and written requests (WhatsApp messages dated **[list dates]**), the landlord has refused to refund the security deposit, nor has he provided any lawful deduction or account of damages.  

---

#### 2.  Legal Basis  

2.1. **Indian Contract Act, 1872 – Section 73**: The rent agreement is a contract. The landlord’s refusal to return the security deposit amounts to a breach of contract, entitling the tenant to claim compensation (i.e., the full deposit amount).  

2.2. **Model Tenancy Act, 2021 – Section 11** (applicable in states that have adopted it): The security deposit for a residential tenancy shall not exceed two months’ rent and must be refunded on surrender of vacant possession, after lawful deductions only. The landlord’s failure to refund the deposit violates this provision.  

2.3. **Limitation Act, 1963**: A civil suit for recovery of money must be filed within three years from the date of cause of action. The present notice is sent well within that period.
